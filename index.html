About me

I grew up in Denver, Colorado, and I've been around sports my whole life. Somewhere along the way I fell in love with the numbers behind the game. A box score, a shot chart, or a draft board can tell its own story if you know how to read it, and finding that story is what pulled me toward sports analytics.

The other half of that is broadcasting. Learning how a live game actually gets put together, from the people running cameras to the editor cutting a feature, has become a big part of my life. I've worked as a SkyCam utility operator on NFL broadcasts and run camera on a live ESPN+ game, and I want to keep building toward work where data and storytelling meet.

At CU Boulder I study Information Science, which lets me work on both sides: understanding the people who use a product, and the data they leave behind. The projects below are a mix of UX research, data analysis, and sports media.
