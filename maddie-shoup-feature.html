The assignment

For my Sports Broadcasting class, our team produced a short feature of about three minutes on a woman athlete. We chose Maddie Shoup and built the piece around her story, from scheduling to the final cut.

How we made it

  • Pre-production: set up shoot dates with Maddie and planned a storyline to shape our interview questions.
  • Production: recorded the sit-down interview, then found and shot the B-roll the story needed.
  • Post-production: as editor, I cut the interview and B-roll together into the finished three-minute feature, pacing it to keep her story moving.

My role

I was the editor. I took the raw interview and B-roll footage and shaped it into the final piece, choosing what to keep, how to sequence it, and how to match visuals to what Maddie was saying. It's the same storytelling instinct I bring to data work: find the thread that matters and cut everything that doesn't serve it.
